1READ

Every field off the receipt or e-FDR, with its place on the page: bank, IFSC, number, amount, dates, lien. OCR for phone photos

›
2CHECK

Security deposit rules: in favour of AICTE, lien marked, amount and tenure enough, branch on RBI's list, not pledged twice

›
3CONFIRM

Ask the bank, never the paper: e-FDR signature, term-deposit API, or a signed one-time link to the branch

›
4DETECT

Tampering signs: white patch over text, a different font on a key value, maturity value that does not fit the rate

›
5MONITOR

Every day: reminders at 90, 60, 30, 7 days, escalation when overdue, bank re-check every 90 days

›
✓RELEASE

Conditions checked, two officials approve, the bank is asked to remove AICTE's lien. Every step on record.
